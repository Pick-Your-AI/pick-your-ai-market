# Market research contributor instructions

Read README.md, CONTRIBUTING.md and the issue's live instructions before preparing work.

- New market research belongs here. Existing Research issues, evidence and decision records stay in place unless a separate migration is explicitly authorized.
- Search both repositories and Project #2 before creating issues, PRs or reports. Record prior work and avoid duplicate backlogs.
- Preserve live assignees, labels, statuses, priorities, workstream fields and dependencies. Team membership is not accepted issue ownership.
- Record primary sources, access dates, claim scope, conditions, confidence and limitations. Do not invent findings, prices or measurements.
- Keep technical component decisions, compatibility, pricebooks and frozen v1 contracts with their Research owners. Use the existing handoff process rather than creating a competing schema.
- Do not commit credentials, personal/customer content, raw datasets, model weights or large evidence downloads.
- Run the checks described in CONTRIBUTING.md. State what remains unverified; do not claim CI, deployment, automatic refresh or research acceptance from this initial repository setup.
